## Overview

The Built Environment (BE) ITM builds upon the strong foundations laid by the earlier Construction and Real Estate (Facilities Management) ITMs. It consolidates previous initiatives into a cohesive, value-chain approach to capture emerging opportunities for the Built Environment sector.

The BE ITM helps build a world-class and resilient BE sector through integrated transformation. It supports effective collaboration amongst stakeholders across the value chain within the sector. This collaboration spans the three key stages of a building's life cycle: planning and design, construction, and operations and maintenance.

## Integrated planning and design

- Drive collaboration across the value chain to enable better planning from the onset and optimise building design for downstream construction and maintenance.
- Create a conducive environment for collaboration and regulatory submissions through common data standards, with a data-rich Building Information Modelling model that is used across the project lifecycle.

## Advanced manufacturing and assembly

- Automate fabrication in factories to improve productivity, quality, and work environment.
- Drive co-location of synergistic construction activities (e.g. aggregate storage, batching plants, precast plants) to optimize land use and streamline logistical processes.

## Sustainable urban systems

- Accelerate our decarbonisation efforts to support our net-zero aspirations and create a more sustainable and liveable built environment.
- Streamline operations and improve maintenance of buildings enabled by Integrated, Aggregated, and Smart Facilities Management.

## Key enablers driving industry-wide transformation

1. Drive value chain transformation
2. Spur innovation and technology to build capable firms
3. Groom a competent workforce with fulfilling careers
4. Strengthen resilience against future disruptions
