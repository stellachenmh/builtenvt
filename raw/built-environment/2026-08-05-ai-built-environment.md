## AI is Transforming the Built Environment

Firms in the Built Environment sector are adopting Artificial Intelligence (AI) to improve productivity, quality, safety, and decision-making. From automating repetitive tasks to analysing complex project data, AI empowers teams to work more efficiently throughout the entire project lifecycle.

As AI technologies evolve, new applications are emerging across planning, design, construction, and facilities management. This page highlights key AI use cases and provides the resources you need to kickstart your AI journey.

## AI Use Cases for the Built Environment

Explore the following emerging AI applications designed to streamline your operations:

- **Knowledge Management & Review:** Use AI to query codes, standards, regulations, and project documents. This speeds up compliance checks, design reviews, and overall quality assurance.
- **AI-Enhanced BIM Workflows:** Enhance Building Information Modelling (BIM) with AI tools that support model checking, documentation, data management, and workflow automation.
- **Tender & Contract Management:** Streamline your procurement process. AI can assist with tender preparation, bid evaluation, contract reviews, quantity take-offs, and cost estimation.
- **Inspections & Progress Analytics:** Leverage AI to analyse site images, videos, scans, and BIM models. This enables smarter site inspections, accurate progress tracking, and delay forecasting.
- **Site Documentation & Reporting:** Automate tedious administrative work. AI can generate site reports, progress updates, issue tracking logs, and management dashboards.
