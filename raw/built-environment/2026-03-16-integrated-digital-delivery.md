## Getting Started with IDD Implementation

Whether your organisation is new to IDD or wants to improve current practices, the resources below can help. They offer clear steps and useful tips for planning and carrying out your projects.

- **Strategic IDD Adoption Guide [PDF, 2.9 MB]** — An overview of IDD fundamentals, key benefits and planning considerations, use cases, and recommended KPI indicators.
- **IDD Requirements Guide [PDF, 453 KB]** — A guide helping clients articulate IDD requirements in tender documents, covering three essential aspects: use cases and technologies, roles and responsibilities, and key deliverables.

## Page context

Integrated Digital Delivery (IDD), one of the key thrusts of the Built Environment Industry Transformation Map (BEITM), is a holistic approach that uses digital technologies to connect all stakeholders involved in a construction project. It streamlines and simplifies every stage of a building's lifecycle — from design to fabrication, construction, and asset management.
