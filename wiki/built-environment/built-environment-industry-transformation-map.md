# Built Environment Industry Transformation Map (ITM)

## Overview

Singapore's Built Environment (BE) Industry Transformation Map is a value-chain framework that builds on the earlier Construction and Real Estate (Facilities Management) ITMs. It consolidates previous initiatives into a cohesive approach to capture emerging opportunities for the sector, with the aim of building a "world-class and resilient" BE sector through integrated transformation.

The framework is organised around collaboration across the three stages of a building's life cycle: **planning and design**, **construction**, and **operations and maintenance**. It is supported by three focus areas, four key enablers and five transformation areas.

## Three focus areas

| Focus area | What it involves |
|------------|------------------|
| Integrated planning and design | Drive value-chain collaboration so planning starts early and building design is optimised for downstream construction and maintenance. Create a conducive environment for collaboration and regulatory submissions through common data standards and a data-rich Building Information Modelling (BIM) model used across the project lifecycle. |
| Advanced manufacturing and assembly | Automate fabrication in factories to improve productivity, quality and the work environment. Co-locate synergistic construction activities such as aggregate storage, batching plants and precast plants to optimise land use and streamline logistics. |
| Sustainable urban systems | Accelerate decarbonisation to support net-zero aspirations and a more sustainable, liveable built environment. Streamline operations and improve building maintenance through Integrated, Aggregated and Smart Facilities Management. |

## Four key enablers

1. Drive value chain transformation.
2. Spur innovation and technology to build capable firms.
3. Groom a competent workforce with fulfilling careers.
4. Strengthen resilience against future disruptions.

## Five transformation areas

The ITM identifies five areas where the sector can pursue the latest advancements:

- Research and Innovation
- Productivity
- Facilities Management
- Manpower
- Procurement

The landing page describes the framework and links to each transformation area for detailed initiatives.

## What the framework emphasises

- **Integration over isolated initiatives.** The BE ITM consolidates earlier Construction and Real Estate (Facilities Management) ITMs into one value-chain approach.
- **Common data standards and BIM.** Digital continuity across the project lifecycle is treated as a foundation for collaboration and regulatory submissions.
- **Factory-based production and co-location.** Automation and co-location of construction activities are positioned as routes to better productivity, quality and land use.
- **Decarbonisation and smart FM.** Sustainability and building operations are part of the same transformation agenda, not separate afterthoughts.
- **Workforce and resilience.** Capable firms, fulfilling careers and resilience against future disruptions are explicit enablers.
